Open with "This is Surface 2 — the internal console. The public site listens to visitors; this is where our team sees what it heard, and decides what happens next. Nothing reaches a customer from here without a human."
visitor review→ scored lead ③→ agent drafts ③→ held at gate ④→ human approves ⑤→ delivered ④ ③–⑤ are the story · cut ⑨–⑩ first if short on time

The run

  1. Login Internal-only, JWT in an httpOnly cookie. Admin & Assessment act; everyone else read-only.
  2. Overview "The daily what-needs-me screen." KPI tiles → click through to Leads.
    ✕ Don't point at Journey distribution — widget hides itself (no endpoint).
  3. Lead detail — Tier 1heart of the demo "Every public-site review becomes a scored lead." Walk the right column: Cockpit (click Next-best-action — journey advances; signals never reach the visitor) → Journey (8 states, reveals ①–④) → Client account → Run agent "Proof" — it queues for approval. Ask the room: "Should the AI ever send this itself?" — no, that's stop 5.
  4. Console Live client conversations. Three sender styles: client / itriX assessment / itriX team. Send at claim level 1 → delivers. Send at level 3 → held. Segue to Approvals.
  5. Approvalsgovernance payoff "Above the threshold, a human waits." Open the draft from stop 3: claim badge, cited chunks. Edit (revises, doesn't send) → Approve & deliver → flip back to Console: it's there. Show an L4/5 draft: "Approve (1 of 2)" — second, different approver. Reject → reason required → audit trail.
  6. Agent runs Every invocation logged: agent, claim level, verdict, AI vs deterministic, duration.
  7. Claim-Cards & Audit "The source of truth agents are checked against." Show the ALPHA Core wording rule. Audit links every decision back to its lead/thread.
  8. Analytics + Pitch Funnel, response time, bottlenecks, pitch-room telemetry. Internal only.
    ✕ Don't compare 30d vs 90d — seeded data spans ~30 days, they look identical.
  9. Pipeline · Follow-up · NDA · Evals · PoCs "The commercial ladder after qualification." Move one card; show the overdue follow-up view.
  10. Reporting · Templates · Settings Monthly reports, reusable templates, team / SLA / governance threshold.
Close with "The deterministic engine decides. The AI only re-words approved content. A human approves anything that carries a claim. That's why we can move fast without over-claiming."

Do not demo

  • Anything "live/realtime" — Console & Approvals poll, sockets aren't wired.
  • "Open thread" / "View lead" cross-links — fields missing from serializers.
  • Journey-distribution widget on Overview — empty, hides itself.
  • Claim-Card editing as a read-only user — form renders but save 403s.
  • "① Client page" preview from RevealLog — no viewer (Surface 1 owns it).
  • Searching/filtering Evaluations or PoCs lists — not built (NDA has it).
  • Analytics 30d vs 90d comparison — seeded data makes them identical.

If they ask

"Is this a chatbot?"
No. The public site has no bot; here, agents draft and humans send.
"Can the AI invent a number?"
No — claims resolve to approved Claim-Cards. L3+ needs citation + human approval; L4/L5 needs two approvers.
"What's live?"
Everything — no mock layer. Unshipped backend routes say "not available yet" instead.

Before you start

  • Deploy! Production still serves a pre-fix build (sidebar/CSS bugs will show).
  • Log in as Admin or Assessment Team — or act buttons show "restricted".
  • One Tier 1 lead open in a spare tab — strongest single screen.
  • Queue one draft in advance so Approvals isn't empty.
